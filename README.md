# Knowledge-Flow

Turn learning notes into clear, memorable knowledge posts, illustrations, and videos.

## Why

As language models become more capable, more of the work of making things can be delegated. That shifts our effort toward deciding what matters, checking whether an explanation is right, and helping other people understand it.

Text is only one way to explain an idea. A concise explanation, a diagram, an interactive web page, or a custom explainer video can each make difficult material easier to understand. With AI-assisted tools, these artifacts can be made for a specific topic and audience, even when they are experimental or intended to be discarded after use.

Knowledge-Flow explores workflows for turning what we learn into useful, engaging explanations.

## A possible flow

```text
Learning notes or keynotes
	-> Flash
	-> GeoGebra
	-> Manim
	-> AI
	-> AppleScript
	-> PocketFlow and other tools
	-> A useful explanation
```

This is a starting point, not a fixed pipeline. Choose the steps that fit the subject and the people who need to understand it. A workflow might include:

- Capture source material, questions, and rough ideas.
- Organize the material into a useful narrative or learning sequence.
- Explore concepts with tools such as GeoGebra.
- Turn dynamic ideas into animations with Manim.
- Use AI to draft, illustrate, code, or connect steps in the workflow.
- Automate repetitive desktop tasks with AppleScript.
- Orchestrate reusable flows with PocketFlow or similar tools.
- Review the result for accuracy, clarity, and usefulness.

## Choose the right format

- **Writing:** Ask for a concise explanation or use a controlled writing style such as ASD-STE100 as a guide to clearer prose.
- **Diagrams and images:** Make relationships, processes, and structure visible at a glance.
- **Interactive pages:** Use HTML to combine explanation, interaction, and visual context in one shareable artifact.
- **Explainer videos:** Combine custom visuals, animation, and narration to teach ideas over time.

The format should serve the idea. AI-generated output still needs human direction and review, especially for factual accuracy and whether the explanation works for its intended audience.

## What belongs here

Knowledge-Flow is a place to collect and experiment with tools, reusable workflows, and examples for creating:

- Knowledge posts and learning materials
- Diagrams and visual explanations
- Interactive web pages
- Animated and narrated explainer videos

The goal is not to build a single heavyweight publishing system. It is to make it practical to create focused, high-quality explanations with the tools available, learn from each attempt, and share what works.
